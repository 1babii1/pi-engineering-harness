# Promote Learnings

Review `.pi/learnings/inbox.md`.

Promote a candidate only if it is:
- repeated or clearly high-impact;
- stable rather than a one-off incident;
- not cheaply rediscoverable;
- specific enough to guide behavior;
- placed in the narrowest appropriate scope (root rule, nested rule, skill, or reference).

Do not grow `AGENTS.md` with generic advice. Remove or merge stale/duplicate rules when promoting new ones.
